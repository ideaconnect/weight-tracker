Contact

Get in touch

Bug reports, questions about your data, trouble with the widget unlock, or a feature you wish the app had. All of it lands in the same inbox, and all of it gets read.

Requests about your data (access, correction, deletion, a copy of your backup) are handled here too. Say which account the request is about and send the message from the email address the account uses, or give that address in the form. A form on its own proves nothing, so we reply to that address first and act once you have answered. The privacy policy lists what we hold and for how long, and the deletion page walks through removing an account.

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a processor acting on our instructions; your name, address and message go to it when you press send, and to nobody else. Nothing is loaded from any third party when this page opens. With JavaScript on, the submit is sent in the background so a rejected message is reported here; with JavaScript off the form posts to Web3Forms directly and still sends.
