2. What the service is

3. Not medical advice

Weight Tracker is a tracking tool. It stores the numbers you give it and does arithmetic on them. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything, and nothing it shows is medical, nutritional or health advice.

  • The plan line, the needed per day and per week figures and the projected finish are straight-line arithmetic from the target and the dates you chose. They do not know whether that pace is sensible for you.
  • The energy figure ("kcal / day") is a rule-of-thumb estimate, computed at roughly 8 400 kcal per kilogram and rounded to the nearest 10 kcal. It is offered as a sense of scale, never as a prescription, and it will be wrong for many people.
  • The trend is a projection of your own recent pace. It is not a prediction.

Decide on a target and a pace with a doctor or a dietitian, especially if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or take medication that affects weight. If eating, food or weight is a source of distress for you, please talk to a professional before using any tracking app, this one included. You use the app and act on its numbers at your own responsibility.

5. Health Connect

The app can read weight from Health Connect and, if you allow it, write your manual entries to it. Both are permissions you grant and can revoke at any time. Please be aware of what the sync does and does not do, because it is deliberate:

  • It fills days you did not type. A weight you typed is never overwritten by a synced one.
  • A day you deleted in the app is never imported again, however many times other apps write it.
  • When several readings exist on one day, the earliest one is taken, on the assumption of a morning weigh-in. An evening reading from a scale that syncs twice a day will be discarded.
  • Background sync runs once a day, only on Android 15 or newer, and only with the extra permission. Otherwise the app syncs when you open it.

We do not control what other apps write to Health Connect, nor Health Connect itself, and we are not responsible for readings that arrive wrong, late or not at all from another app or device.

6. Backup and restore

Backup keeps one snapshot of your entries, deleted days and plan per account. A new upload replaces the previous one; there is no history of earlier snapshots and no partial or per-day recovery.

  • Uploads happen automatically while the switch is on. Restore happens only when you ask, and it replaces what is on the phone.
  • If two phones share an account, an upload that would replace a snapshot the current phone did not write is stopped and you are asked which copy to keep. The app does not merge. Whichever you choose wins, and the other is gone.
  • Backup is a convenience, not an archive. If your history matters to you, also export it as a CSV from Settings now and then. We are not liable for a snapshot you replaced, cleared or overwrote by choosing "replace" in the dialogue above.
