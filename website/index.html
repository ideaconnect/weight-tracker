One plan, three ways to set it

Pick a target weight, then choose what stays fixed: a date, a weekly pace, or no deadline at all. The app works out the rest: the daily rate, what is needed per week, the projected finish and the daily energy deficit it amounts to, rounded to the nearest 10 kcal.

Gaining works too

A reminder with real numbers

Every day at the time you choose, the notification says where you stand and what you weighed yesterday. Type today's number straight into it, or snooze it for an hour.

Health Connect, the polite way

Your scale, your watch and your other fitness apps write to Health Connect. Weight Tracker reads from it and fills the gaps. The rules are simple and they never change:

  • Manual entries always win. A synced reading never overwrites a number you typed.
  • Deleted days stay deleted. Remove a day and the sync will not sneak it back in.
  • Morning wins. Several readings on one day? The earliest one counts.
  • Write-back is optional. Let the app write your manual entries to Health Connect, or not.
  • Sync on open, or once a day in the background on Android 15 and newer.

Offline by default, backup by choice

Everything works with no account and no network. If you want your plan and history to survive a new phone, create an account with an email address and a 6-digit code, switch backup on, and uploads happen by themselves. Restoring is always by hand, and one tap clears the cloud copy.

A finish line

Cross your target and you get the trophy screen, once per plan. It is the one moment the app congratulates you. The rest of the time the numbers do the encouraging.

History you can read

Every entry, week by week, with the weekly average, the change from the day before, and a badge that says whether it was typed or synced. Tap a row to edit or delete it.

Your data, as a file

Export the whole history as a CSV whenever you like. It is your data. There is also a delete-everything button, and it does exactly that.

Kilograms or pounds, black or white

Switch units at any time; the stored numbers never change. Dark mode is true black so AMOLED screens switch pixels off, and it is painted before the first frame so there is no white flash on launch.

How it works

Three steps. Then just the one, every morning.

Log a weight

Tap the plus, type the number on the big keypad, save. Yesterday's figure sits right there so you know you got it right. Or connect Health Connect and let your scale do the typing.

Set a goal

A target weight, and either a date, a pace, or nothing at all. The plan starts from your latest entry, so the line begins the day you decide.

Watch the line

Each new entry redraws the chart, the plan screen and every widget. When the line crosses the target, the trophy screen is waiting. Once. Then set the next one.

Privacy

Your weight is nobody's business. Including ours.

Weight Tracker keeps your entries on your phone. There is no analytics, no tracking, and Android's own cloud backup of the app is switched off, so nothing leaves the device unless you turn something on.

  • Backup is a switch you flip, and one tap deletes the cloud copy again.
  • Health Connect data is used to draw your chart and for nothing else. Never for ads, never sold.
  • Delete all data means all data. Delete account means the account and its backup.

Questions

Things people ask.

Do I need an account?

No. The app is built to work with no account and no network. An account exists for one reason: to back up your plan and history so a new phone can pick up where the old one left off. It is an email address, a password and a 6-digit code, and you can delete it from inside the app.

What does the widget unlock cost?

Google Play shows the price in your currency before you buy. It is a single, one-time purchase through Google Play's billing, not a subscription. It unlocks the six widgets and removes the banner ad for good.

Does it work with my scale?

If your scale, watch or fitness app writes weight to Health Connect, yes. Weight Tracker reads weight from Health Connect and fills in the days you did not type. Your own entries always win, and a day you deleted is never imported again. It can also write your manual entries back, if you let it.

What happens when I fall behind?

The colour changes from green to amber. That is all. No red, no streak lost, no message telling you off. The plan screen still shows what is needed per day and per week to get back, and the trend line shows where your current pace lands you.

Can I set a goal to gain weight?

Yes. Set a target above your current weight and the same maths runs with the sign reversed. Ahead still means the good side of the plan line.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. Background sync with Health Connect while the app is closed needs Android 15; on older versions the app syncs every time you open it, which is what most people want anyway.

Is this medical advice?

No. Weight Tracker is a tracking tool. It plots the numbers you give it and does arithmetic on them. The energy figure is a rule-of-thumb estimate, not a prescription. Talk to a doctor or a dietitian before changing your weight in a hurry, and if eating or weight is a source of distress, please speak to a professional first.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, as a CSV file from Settings, whenever you like. And you can delete everything with one button, also from Settings.
